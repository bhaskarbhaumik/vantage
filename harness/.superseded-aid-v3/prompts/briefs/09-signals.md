# Brief 09 — `signals` · Wave A · Block 0900–0999 · Owns §13 Current Signals, Initiatives & Intent Intelligence

You cover **what is happening right now and what it implies** — the 12-to-24-month forward-looking
layer. Almost everything you touch is `ANNOUNCED` or `OBSERVED-SIGNAL`; almost nothing is
`CONFIRMED`. Holding that line is the whole value of this section.

Output: `sections/13-signals.md` · fragment `state/fragments/signals.json`.

## Boundary with `strategy` (§9)

`strategy` owns the **stated strategy and the programme register**. You own **dated signals in the
last 12–24 months** and their forward implication. Where a programme appears in both, cite their
claim rather than restating it, and add only the timing and the signal read. Coordinate by
`handoff`; duplicated programme registers are a V10g finding.

## §13.3 Hiring & Talent Signals — the discipline section

`T-13.3` + `C-13.3`.

- **State the posting window explicitly** ("847 postings sampled from careers site and ATS JSON,
  2026-05-01 to 2026-08-29"). An undated posting count is meaningless.
- **Classify every technology mention** per `evidence-policy.md` §6. A technology in a job
  requirement is `SKILL-PREFERENCE`, ceiling `OBSERVED-SIGNAL`. It is never a stack fact — that is
  `technology`'s call in §8.1, on different evidence.
- Role density by function and geography → investment priorities, **as `OBSERVED-SIGNAL`**.
- Note what the postings do *not* show. A company with no data-engineering postings in a quarter
  when it announced a data platform is a finding.

Detail behind this subsection goes to §21.5, written by `appendices` — hand off the raw breakdown.

## §13.7 Procurement & Contract-Renewal Intelligence

RFP channels and portals · framework agreements and contract vehicles · **public contract awards
with their expiry dates** (public sector, where the award record states them) · renewal evidence and

> **Never invent a renewal date** (V9d, BLOCKER). Record the **evidenced signal** and the **window
> it implies**: "five-year award dated 2022-03; award record states a 2027-03 end date [S-0912]" is
> evidence. "Contract likely up for renewal in 2027" with no award record is fabrication.
> **Empty is a valid render** and is common for commercial targets.

This subsection drives §18.6 timing. An empty §13.7 means §18.6 leans on fiscal calendar and
leadership changes instead — which is honest, and which you should say.

## Traps

- **A press release is `ANNOUNCED`.** However specific and however recent.
- **Do not aggregate postings across an unstated window** — hiring is seasonal.
- **Google News RSS and similar unofficial feeds are never a sole source** for a material claim.
- **An analyst price target is not a company fact.** Attribute it.
- **A conference talk abstract is a plan, not a deployment.**
