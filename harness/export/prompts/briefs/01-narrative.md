# Brief — narrative

You go first. Everything downstream reads what you write.

## Read

- The title block and the "Read before use" banner: Overall Confidence (EQI), verification
  result, conditions, identifier traps.
- The whole of **§1** (§1.1–§1.7), including every verification erratum.
- **§18 Opportunity**, **§19 Account strategy**, **§20 Playbook** (especially the §20.6 action
  register).
- **§21.8** (verification findings and disclosed failures) and the gap rows of **§21.11** that §1
  cites.
- **§0.1** run contract: what was NOT ATTACHED (Capability Pack, Account-Team Input Sheet), and the
  account context.
- Skim §3–§17: each section's opening verdict.

## Decide

Write `$PLAN_DIR/story.md` — Markdown, 600–1,200 words — answering:

1. **The verdict, in one sentence**, with its confidence and posture (e.g. Nurture / Develop).
2. **The tension** — the thing that makes this a decision rather than an obvious yes or no. This
   is the spine of the deck.
3. **The five things that change the next move**, ranked by what a reader would do differently,
   each with its claim references and status.
4. **What is not there** — the gaps that gate spend (headline) versus merely untidy (footnote).
5. **What must not be over-claimed** — suppressed composites, `[ESTIMATED]` figures, LOW-confidence
   claims doing load-bearing work, ANNOUNCED-not-closed events, refuted claims, errata that change
   a reading. Downstream agents and QA check against this list.
6. **The shape of each collateral** — deck (what leads, what is cut, roughly how many slides, which
   two or three dossier charts/figures earn a slide), whitepaper (which sections carry weight, what
   the abstract says), microsite (whether the default grouping fits).
7. **Visual direction** — the 3–5 stock-image descriptions that fit this account (cover, dividers),
   and any icon you would use beyond the defaults.

## How to judge

You are not summarising. You are deciding what a pursuit lead should do on Monday, out of what the
dossier established. If the honest answer is "not worth pursuit budget yet, and here is the one
question that would change that", say exactly that. Collateral that manufactures enthusiasm out of
a Nurture verdict is a liability.
